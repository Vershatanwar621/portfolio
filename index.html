DESIGN METHODOLOGY

HIERARCHY, COLOUR & TYPE

Three core design principles applied to every packaging label, social post, corporate brochure, and digital interface above.

PRINCIPLE 01

Visual Hierarchy

Size, weight and placement tell the viewer what to read first, second and last. One dominant hero element, one supporting message, and one decisive action.

Headline First
Supporting Message Second
Technical specifications and fine print last
Decisive Action ↗
PRINCIPLE 02

Colour Strategy

A dominant neutral, a secondary supporting tone, and one sharp accent for what truly matters. Palettes are engineered for contrast that stays legible in crowded feeds.

60% Base Canvas · 30% Supporting Tone · 10% Sharp Accent
PRINCIPLE 03

Typography Discipline

Pair an expressive, monumental display face with a disciplined, quiet text face. High contrast in scale and weight generates visual rhythm while preserving readability.

Aa
Syne & Archivo Black for Display
Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono set with generous breathing room.
